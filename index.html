01 / THE CHALLENGE

A follow-up six months away is easy to forget.

A pediatrician recommends a return visit, vaccine, lab, or specialist appointment. Months pass. Life gets busy. Families may miss recommended care—and practices lose the opportunity to reconnect with patients who should have returned.

↗

Long gaps between visits

A six- or eight-month follow-up can slip off the calendar, even when it matters for a child’s care.

♡

Parents need one trusted connection

Parents want a convenient way to ask routine questions, see relevant care information, and know what comes next.

◎

Missed visits affect practices, too

When recommended visits are missed, clinics can lose continuity with families and revenue from visits that might otherwise have occurred.

02 / OUR SOLUTION

A lasting bridge between pediatric clinics and families.

CareBridge is designed as the ongoing parent–clinic connection: a place for timely follow-up reminders, everyday communication, and useful pediatric care information. Clinics stay engaged with families while parents stay on top of their children’s care.

06 / OUR STORY

A simple belief sparked CareBridge.

CareBridge began as a student-led initiative around a familiar problem: a child leaves the pediatrician with a plan, but the next visit may be months away. What if parents and clinics had an easier way to stay connected until that next step?

That idea is now being explored through a real pediatric clinic pilot in Johns Creek. Our vision is to make CareBridge a trusted connection for reminders, everyday questions, and access to helpful care information—while supporting stronger continuity and sustainable pediatric practices.

Made with care. Built to make a difference.
